# LocalSend — send a photo to your phone

**What it is:** LocalSend sends files between your own devices on the same Wi-Fi, no cloud and no account.
**For:** Anyone moving photos and files between their computer and phone.
**Hook:** Open on the payoff: the photo already on the phone.

## The flow

- LocalSend is open on the PC, a phone nearby
- Send → File: pick a photo
- The phone shows up under Nearby devices: click it
- The phone accepts; the photo arrives

## Checks

- Length: 12–22 s
- "File" by 6 s
- "Pixel 8" by 12 s
