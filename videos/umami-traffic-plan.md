# Umami — where did that spike come from?

**What it is:** Umami is privacy-first web analytics: traffic, sources and behaviour, without cookies.
**For:** People who run a website and want to know what brings visitors in.
**Hook:** Open on the answer: the visitors from Hacker News, the spike in the chart.

## The flow

- Taskly's dashboard in Umami
- Last 30 days: one day stands out
- Sources: click news.ycombinator.com
- The dashboard filters to it: the spike was Hacker News

## Checks

- Length: 12–22 s
- "Last 30 days" by 6 s
- "news.ycombinator.com" by 14 s
