# Rottoways: before and after

**What it is:** Rottoways is a design system pack you paste into Lovable, Cursor or Claude so an AI-built landing page stops looking like AI slop.
**For:** Founders and indie builders with a vibecoded landing page.

## The flow

- The pitch
- Before: the page as AI tools build it
- After: the same page with the pack
- Your copy stays, the design changes
- How you use it: renovate or start new
- What's in the pack
- The price

## Checks

- Length: 25–50 s
- "Before" by 10 s
- "After" by 22 s
