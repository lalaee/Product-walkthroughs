# Gemini

This repo makes product walkthrough videos of apps: it runs the real app, scripts one flow,
records it with Recordly (Recorder-2) and reviews it frame by frame.

When asked for a walkthrough, demo or tutorial video of an app (or of a repo), follow
`skills/product-walkthrough/SKILL.md` step by step, and read the files in
`skills/product-walkthrough/references/` when that skill points to them.
